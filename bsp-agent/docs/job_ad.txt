Your challenge

Build a working prototype that demonstrates how AI could solve a genuine problem for our business.

We particularly welcome original ideas.

We're not interested in a presentation explaining what you could build. We want to see something you've actually created.

What to submit

Please send us:

A link to your working prototype that we can test, or a short screen recording showing it working.
A brief explanation of the business problem it solves.
A short explanation of how you built it, including the AI tools and technologies used.
What you would improve next if you had access to our internal systems.
Your solution doesn't need to be perfect or commercially ready. We're more interested in your thinking, problem-solving ability and technical skills.

We'll review submissions based on creativity, functionality, technical ability and the potential value to our business.
